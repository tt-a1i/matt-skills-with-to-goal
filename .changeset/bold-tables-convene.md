Add `roundtable`, a user-invoked skill that pressure-tests an already-formed decision, plan, or proposal by convening parallel sub-agents arguing deliberately opposed perspectives — independent statements, anonymous cross-review, then a Chair's synthesis that reports the verdict while preserving consensus and dissent. The complement of grilling: grilling sharpens an idea that isn't formed yet, the roundtable attacks a settled motion. Promoted into `skills/engineering/` with a plugin entry, README and Engineering-bucket listings, an `ask-matt` route, and a docs page at `docs/engineering/roundtable.md`.
